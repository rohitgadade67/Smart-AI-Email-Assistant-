Privacy Policy for Smart AI Email Assistant

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Smart AI Email Assistant. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application interacts with your data when you use the service.

1. Information We Access

Smart AI Email Assistant requires access to your email notifications to monitor unread incoming emails and provide on-screen alerts. The tool only checks email metadata and status to determine unread messages.

2. Data Usage and Storage

Our application operates locally or on your authorized workspace. We do not sell, store, trade, or share your personal emails or sensitive user data with third parties. All processing is solely used to notify you of unread emails.

3. Third-Party Services

Smart AI Email Assistant integrates with Google APIs to deliver notifications. Any use of Google API data strictly adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Security

We do not collect passwords or unauthorized credentials. Communication with official APIs happens over secure, standard authentication protocols.
